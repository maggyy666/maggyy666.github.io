Welcome to My Portfolio!

I’m a Data Engineer @ DATABROS, working from Kraków, currently pursuing an MSc in Data Science at Cracow University of Technology. On a daily basis I work on a large-scale big data platform — building and maintaining data pipelines and the infrastructure behind them, with a focus on scalable, reliable solutions.

I hold the CKAD (Certified Kubernetes Application Developer) certification since June 2026.

A big part of my work is onboarding large-scale data sources onto the platform — analyzing new datasets, designing ingestion flows and making sure the data lands clean, validated and ready for downstream consumers. I work closely with data providers and stakeholders to smoothly integrate new sources into existing processing workflows, keeping pipelines reliable and maintainable as the platform grows.

Currently I'm deepening my expertise in Apache Airflow, S3-compatible object storage and ClickHouse to deliver even more scalable and resilient data platforms.
